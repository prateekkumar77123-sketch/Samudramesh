01 · Problem

The drain is the highway.

Mumbai's stormwater drains, nullahs and creeks carry rainwater out to sea — and anything dropped on the street rides along. Bags, sachets, cups and bottles enter at the kerb; sunlight, abrasion and tides then shred them into microplastics that no net at the beach can recover. Intercepting at the drain, early and continuously, is the cheapest point to win.

1

Entry

Street litter washes into catch-pits and road drains during rain.

2

Transit

Nullahs push it along; tide reverses flow and pushes debris back and forth.

3

Fragmentation

UV and friction break film and foam into microplastics.

4

Sea

Creek mouths release it to coastal water, where recovery is nearly impossible.

02 · Mumbai's water network

Where Samudramesh sits.

Monsoon safety

Self-clearing screens with bypass flaps: if flow rises, the screen opens so water is never dammed. Water level sensors trigger it automatically.

Tidal safety

Two-way design: traps hold debris whether flow moves seaward or backward on the incoming tide.

04 · Detection

Seeing plastic.

Samudramesh needs to know what is in the water. Four ways to detect it, from phone to hardware:

Phone camera + AI

Snap debris or a drain; a lightweight object-detection model (YOLO / MobileNet-SSD) labels bag, bottle, wrapper. Citizens become sensors.

Fixed CCTV on drains

Edge computer counts and classifies floating items per hour, giving a live litter index per nullah.

NIR spectroscopy

Near-infrared light tells PET from PE from PP by spectral signature, for sorting at the belt.

Microplastic imaging

Nile-red dye fluorescence under blue light, plus FTIR/Raman, for sub-5mm fragments in water samples.

Try the phone-camera demo

Upload or capture a photo. Demo mode: this page runs a scripted scan so you can see the flow; the real model (trained on Mumbai drain images) runs on-device in the full build.

05 · Workflow

From drain to second life.

A

Intercept

Inlet screens, nullah booms and creek-mouth traps catch macro-plastic.

B

Sense

Cameras + level sensors classify load and watch flood risk.

C

Capture fines

Fine mesh and settling zones hold microplastic fragments.

D

Sort

AI + NIR separate polymer types.

E

Upcycle

Pellets, tiles, kerbstones, fuel feedstock — nothing back to the sea.
